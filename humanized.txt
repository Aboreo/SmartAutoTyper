E. Long-Term Strategy for the Stock Market
Based on everything I experienced and learned during this project, my long-term strategy for the stock market now has many new ideas.
First, I would use a long-term, buy-and-hold philosophy for index funds and stable individual stocks. It is proven in general and in the simulation that it is really hard to beat index funds over long periods of time. As a young person, by investing in these index funds I would allow compound interest on annual returns of 7–10% to turn small contributions into decent wealth. 
Second, the main individual stocks I would buy are stocks that are mainly focused in sectors that I believe in their growth (Tech, Defense, etc.) for the long-term. Companies like Nvidia, Apple, and Google are right in the middle of ongoing technological shifts that I believe will fundamentally change the world. AI, cloud computing, robotics, and the semiconductor supply chain are areas where I would concentrate on individual stocks. 
Third, I would stay informed but not obsessive. Checking stock prices every day and reacting to every headline, which I found doing during this project, would lead to unnecessary stress and possible rash decisions. Rather, I would like to be focused on the long-term. By buying long-term stocks and not rushing to buy temporary rising stocks, I think I have a chance to enjoy the benefits of the stock market.
